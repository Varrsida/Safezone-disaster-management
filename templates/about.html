SIH 2026 Problem Statement ID: SIH26191

SafeZone: Hazard Assessment & Relocation Planning System

An educational decision-support prototype developed for the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) / Ministry of Home Affairs challenge to assist authorities in identifying vulnerable habitations, scoring multi-hazard risk, evaluating safer recipient grounds, auditing carrying capacity, and prioritizing immediate relocation needs.

1. Problem Definition & Objectives

Settlements located in floodplains, landslide-prone hillsides, cloudburst catchments, and coastal erosion zones face severe recurrent risks to life and infrastructure. Traditional response often lacks integrated decision tools to rapidly evaluate where residents can be safely moved based on real-time carrying capacity.

Core System Objectives:

  • Identify and index settlements exposed to single or multi-hazards.
  • Calculate transparent, multi-factor risk scores (0–100) and classify risk categories (Low, Moderate, High, Critical).
  • Audit candidate safe zones for available land area, carrying capacity, utility connections (water/power), and distance.
  • Rank recipient sites and generate actionable relocation plans for emergency decision-makers.

2. Risk Scoring & Machine Learning Methodology

A. Mathematical Composite Risk Score

The risk score (0–100) is calculated using a transparent weighted linear model:

Risk Score = 0.25*(Hazard Exp) + 0.15*(Precipitation) + 0.12*(Slope) + 0.15*(Vuln Pop %) + 0.12*(Hist Freq) + 0.10*(Road Isolation) + 0.06*(Emergency Dist) + 0.05*(Infra Risk)

3. Carrying Capacity & Relocation Matching Engine

Carrying capacity for safe zones is dynamically tracked in SQLite using:

Remaining Capacity = Designed Capacity - Current Occupancy
Utilization Percentage = (Current Occupancy / Designed Capacity) * 100

The site matching algorithm filters candidate locations whose remaining capacity is greater than or equal to the habitation population requiring relocation, and ranks remaining sites using Haversine distance calculations and suitability scores.

4. System Limitations

  • Simulated Data: All habitations, safe zones, and hazard records are synthetically generated for prototype demonstration.
  • Synthetic ML Training: Machine learning model is trained on synthetic data distributions and does not reflect official geological surveys.
  • Field Verification Required: Output recommendations are decision-support aids and require physical field audit by certified engineers prior to executing real-world evacuations.
  • Not Certified: Prototype is not certified for operational emergency use.

5. Future Enhancements & Integration Roadmap

  • Integration with official Bhuvan / ISRO / Survey of India GIS layers.
  • Real-time satellite precipitation feeds (IMD API) and river gauge telemetry.
  • Drone-based high-resolution elevation and landslide slope modeling.
  • Evacuation route optimization using OpenStreetMap road network graphs.
  • Mobile application with offline field survey sync capabilities.

Educational Prototype Notice

This system is developed for academic and demonstration purposes using simulated data. Risk classifications, safe-location assessments, and relocation recommendations are decision-support outputs only. They must not be treated as official hazard assessments or used as a substitute for field verification and decisions by authorized disaster-management and engineering professionals.
